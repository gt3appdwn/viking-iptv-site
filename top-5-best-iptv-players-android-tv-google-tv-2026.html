Top 5 Best IPTV Players for Android TV & Google TV in 2026

Top 5 Best IPTV Players for Android TV & Google TV in 2026

Streaming live TV over the internet has become mainstream, and Android TV and Google TV devices are the perfect canvas for a seamless experience. In 2026 the market is crowded, but a handful of apps stand out for performance, UI design, and compatibility with Viking IPTV playlists. Below we review the top five, walk you through a step‑by‑step installation, and give troubleshooting tips to keep your streams smooth.

1. IPTV Smarters Pro

Why it ranks #1

  • Native 4K HDR support – essential for modern 4K TVs.
  • Multi‑user profiles let family members keep separate watchlists.
  • Built‑in EPG (Electronic Program Guide) that updates automatically.
  • Supports both M3U and JSON playlists, perfect for Viking IPTV.

Installation on Android TV / Google TV

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your TV.
  2. Search for “IPTV Smarters Pro” and select the official app.
  3. Click Install and wait for the download to finish.
  4. Launch the app and tap ‘Add Playlist’. Choose ‘M3U URL’ and paste your Viking IPTV link.
  5. Enter your username and password if required, then tap ‘Add’.
  6. Enjoy live channels, VOD, and catch‑up TV from the main screen.

Troubleshooting common issues

  • Buffering or freeze: Go to Settings → Player → Enable hardware decoding and set Buffer Size to 8 seconds.
  • EPG not loading: Verify the EPG URL in the playlist file; replace it with a current source from Viking IPTV’s support page.
  • App crashes on startup: Clear cache (Settings → Apps → IPTV Smarters Pro → Storage → Clear cache) and ensure your TV firmware is up to date.

2. TiviMate (Premium)

Why it ranks #2

  • Optimized UI for remote‑control navigation.
  • Playlist merging – combine multiple M3U sources into one view.
  • Advanced channel grouping and custom icons.
  • Supports catch‑up, series, and movie libraries.

Setup guide

  1. Purchase the Premium version from the Play Store (one‑time fee).
  2. After installation, open TiviMate and select ‘Add Playlist’.
  3. Enter your Viking IPTV M3U URL and save.
  4. Navigate to Settings → Player → Enable ‘Force HW decoding’ for smoother 4K playback.
  5. Use the ‘Edit’ button to rename channels or assign custom logos from the Viking IPTV portal.

Tips for a stable experience

  • Use a wired Ethernet connection whenever possible; Wi‑Fi can cause occasional packet loss on high‑bitrate streams.
  • Set the app’s ‘Auto‑Update EPG’ to once per day to avoid stale program data.
  • If you see a black screen, toggle ‘Enable HDR’ off and on in the player settings.

3. GSE Smart IPTV

Key strengths

  • Cross‑platform sync – start a channel on your phone, continue on TV.
  • Supports subtitles in multiple languages.
  • Built‑in VPN toggle (requires separate VPN subscription).
  • Free version available with limited channel count; Premium unlocks unlimited playlists.

Step‑by‑step installation

  1. Search “GSE Smart IPTV” in the Play Store and install the free version.
  2. Open the app, tap the ‘+’ icon, then choose ‘Add M3U URL’.
  3. Paste your Viking IPTV link, name the playlist “Viking 4K”, and save.
  4. To enable subtitles, go to Settings → Subtitles → Auto‑load and select your preferred language.
  5. For premium features, purchase the upgrade within the app and re‑login.

Common problems and fixes

  • No audio: Switch the audio output mode from ‘Stereo’ to ‘Surround’ in Player Settings.
  • Channel list not updating: Refresh the playlist manually by pulling down on the channel list screen.
  • App freezes after long usage: Restart the TV or clear the app cache weekly.

4. Perfect Player IPTV

What makes it stand out

  • Highly customizable UI – skins, grid size, and channel icons.
  • Supports both M3U and XSPF playlist formats.
  • Integrated VPN support for geo‑restricted channels.
  • Lightweight; ideal for older Android TV models.

Installation & configuration

  1. Download “Perfect Player IPTV�� from the Play Store.
  2. Launch the app, go to Settings → General → Playlist type and select ‘M3U URL’.
  3. Enter the Viking IPTV URL, then go to Settings → EPG → EPG type and choose ‘XML URL’ (use the EPG link supplied by Viking).
  4. Enable ‘Hardware decoding’ under Player → Decoder for smoother 4K playback.
  5. Save and return to the main screen – channels should populate automatically.

Quick troubleshooting

  • If channels appear blank, verify the URL protocol (http vs https) – some TV firmware blocks insecure connections.
  • For laggy navigation, reduce the number of channels displayed per page in Settings → UI → Grid size.
  • When VPN disconnects, re‑activate it from Settings → VPN and test with a geo‑blocked channel.

5. Kodi with IPTV Simple Client

Why Kodi still matters in 2026

  • Open‑source platform with thousands of add‑ons for extra functionality.
  • Full control over video output, audio passthrough, and subtitle rendering.
  • Can run on Android TV, Google TV, and even on a Raspberry Pi for a dedicated box.
  • Integrates seamlessly with Viking IPTV via the IPTV Simple Client add‑on.

Setup guide for Android TV

  1. Install “Kodi” from the Play Store (official version).
  2. Open Kodi, go to Settings (gear icon) → Add‑ons → Install from repository → PVR clients → IPTV Simple Client.
  3. Select ‘Enable’, then under ‘Location’ choose ‘M3U URL’ and paste your Viking IPTV link.
  4. Enter the EPG URL in the ‘XMLTV URL’ field (provided by Viking). Save and restart Kodi.
  5. Navigate to TV → Live TV; you’ll see the full channel list ready to stream.

Typical issues and remedies

  • Black screen on 4K content: Enable ‘Allow hardware acceleration – DXVA2’ in Settings → Player → Videos.
  • Audio out of sync: Adjust ‘Audio delay’ in Settings → Player �� Audio.
  • Add‑on updates fail: Clear Kodi’s cache (Settings → System → Clear cache) and retry.

General troubleshooting checklist for all players

  1. Check your internet speed – at least 15 Mbps for 4K streams.
  2. Prefer wired Ethernet; if using Wi‑Fi, ensure a 5 GHz connection with strong signal.
  3. Update the TV firmware and the IPTV app to the latest version.
  4. Verify the playlist URL is active – open it in a browser on a PC.
  5. Restart the TV after any app installation or playlist change.

Conclusion

All five players listed above support 4K HDR, EPG integration, and the M3U format that powers Viking IPTV’s channel lineup. Choose the one that matches your workflow – whether you value a polished UI (IPTV Smarters Pro), advanced channel grouping (TiviMate), cross‑device sync (GSE Smart IPTV), lightweight performance (Perfect Player), or ultimate customizability (Kodi). Follow the setup steps, keep your network healthy, and you’ll enjoy uninterrupted streaming on your Android TV or Google TV in 2026.

FAQ

  • Do I need a VPN to use Viking IPTV on Android TV? A VPN is not mandatory, but it helps protect privacy and can unblock geo‑restricted channels. Many of the players above include built‑in VPN toggles.
  • Can I use the same playlist on multiple devices? Yes. Your Viking IPTV subscription allows simultaneous streams on up to 5 devices; just add the same M3U URL to each app.
  • Which player offers the best 4K HDR performance? IPTV Smarters Pro and TiviMate Premium are currently the most reliable for native 4K HDR playback on Android TV hardware.
  • How often should I update my playlist? Viking IPTV refreshes its channel list weekly. Re‑import the URL or enable auto‑update in the app’s EPG settings to stay current.
  • Is there a free option that still supports 4K? Perfect Player and the free tier of GSE Smart IPTV can play 4K streams, but they may lack premium UI features and unlimited playlist support.
